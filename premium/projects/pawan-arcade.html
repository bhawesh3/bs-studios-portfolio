01 / THE BUSINESS

Local store.
Digital-first discovery.

For an electronics retailer, customers often compare products, features, prices and current offers before visiting a store. Social media and local search can help keep the business discoverable and make product communication easier.

BS STUDIOS' project scope covered the digital marketing mix: social media management, creative production, paid promotion support and local online presence.

02 / WHAT WE DID

One connected plan.
Multiple touchpoints.

Social media management

Planning the content calendar, preparing posts and reels, writing captions and maintaining a consistent brand presence.

Creative design & offers

Promotional creatives for products, seasonal campaigns, festive offers and retail announcements.

Reels & product content

Short-form content concepts and editing to communicate product features, use cases and store updates.

Meta Ads support

Campaign planning and setup support to promote selected products, offers and local store messages to relevant audiences.

Google Business Profile & local SEO

Local presence support so customers can find business information, location details and contact options through Search and Maps.

Lead and enquiry journey

Clear calls to action designed to encourage calls, WhatsApp conversations and in-store enquiries, with tracking recommendations.
